# Simone Guarneri — Landing

Personal landing page for Simone Guarneri: short-term rental operator and software builder in Campania. It presents his work and turns visitors into conversations.

## Brands and products

**Dorotea Housing**:
The short-term rental brand Simone built from scratch in 2023 with Tommaso Apicella: homes on the Amalfi Coast, in Salerno and in Milan.
_Avoid_: Dorotea, Dorotea Homes

**Quattro Houses**:
A network of 50+ short-term rental homes around Vietri sul Mare and Baronissi that Simone collaborates with, managing a few of its Homes.
_Avoid_: Quattro, Quattro Case

**Casabase**:
Simone's in-development software layer on top of channel managers that optimizes and extends them.

**Casadata**:
Simone's in-development data aggregator for short-term rental market research.

## People and things

**Owner**:
A property owner who might hand a Home to Simone for management. The primary audience of the page. "Property owner" is fine in page copy.
_Avoid_: Landlord, client

**Home**:
A single short-term rental unit, as counted in Brand figures. "Property" is an accepted synonym in page copy (property management, property manager).
_Avoid_: Listing (that is the ad on a booking platform, not the unit)

**Brand figures**:
The headline numbers shown for a brand (homes, locations, revenue, growth, occupancy). Each figure is either published or withheld; invented "sample" numbers are never published.
_Avoid_: Stats, sample figures
